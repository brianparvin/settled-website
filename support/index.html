Frequently asked questions

How do I get Settled?

Settled is an iPhone app distributed through the App Store. Sign up for updates and we’ll email you when it’s available to download in your region.

How does a case work?

You open a case, pick a category, and make your argument. The other person adds their side. You then invite friends to serve as the jury, or ask the AI Judge for an instant take. Once the votes are in, Settled shows the verdict, the vote split, and the jurors’ reasoning.

Who can see my cases?

Cases are visible only to the people involved and the jurors they invite. There is no public feed.

Is a Settled verdict legally binding?

No. Settled is for friendly, everyday disagreements. Verdicts are opinions for entertainment and are not legal advice or a legal decision. For legal matters, please talk to a qualified attorney.

How do I report a case or a user?

In the app, use the Report option on any case, comment, or profile. You can also email hello@settled.social with the details. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content that breaks our Terms of Use.

How do I block someone?

Open the person’s profile in the app and choose Block. They won’t be able to invite you to cases, add you as a juror, or see your activity.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and the personal information tied to it, as described in our Privacy Policy. You can also email us to request deletion.
